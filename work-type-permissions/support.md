# Support

Email **keelwright.apps@gmail.com**. First reply within 2 business days, Monday to
Friday.

To help us help you quickly, include:

- your site URL (for example, `yourcompany.atlassian.net`);
- the space key and the work type involved;
- what you expected and what happened, with the exact error message;
- the result of **Check a person** for the person affected, if relevant.

Never send passwords or API tokens.

Before writing, you may find the answer in the [documentation](index.md),
especially [Limits](index.md#limits) and the [FAQ](index.md#faq).
